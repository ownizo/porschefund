The petrol 718 is gone. The electric one is still a rumour with headlights

5 November 2026 · by the Portuguese dad who still wants a Taycan

The petrol Boxster and Cayman left production in October 2025. If you wanted the cheap(ish), light, mid-engine Porsche, you are now shopping leftovers and classifieds, which is a polite way of saying the door closed while you were tying a shoelace.

The replacement is electric. Porsche has said there will not be a new petrol one. The car people have sat in, as a prototype, keeps the battery behind the seats, where the engine used to live, which is either poetry or a very expensive joke about ghosts.

What they have not given us, as I write this, is a price, a power figure, or a date more precise than “you will see it in 2027, and 2028 is the first full year it is actually built.” So the 718 is currently a promise with a windscreen. I know the feeling. This whole fund is a promise with a progress bar.

I hope they get it right. A small electric Porsche that still feels like a toy you stole from the future would be good for the species. It will not be my first Porsche. I have already sworn the oath, in public, on a webpage, which is the modern version of swearing it on a bible. Taycan. Birthday. Or a charity gets the money. No third ending where I quietly buy a scooter.

Not a road test. I have not driven these cars. This is a diary from a man raising money, one euro at a time, for a Taycan by 2 August 2027. Not affiliated with Porsche AG.
