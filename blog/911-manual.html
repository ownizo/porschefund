The manual 911 came back and my left foot filed a complaint

13 August 2026 · by the Portuguese dad who still wants a Taycan

In August, Porsche put a clutch pedal back in the 911 Carrera S for North America. Six speeds. Three pedals. A whole paragraph of nostalgia written by people who can afford nostalgia.

I read it on my phone while a baby slept on my arm and felt, briefly, like a man from a film. Then the baby woke up and I was a man from a kitchen again.

The 911 did not need my sympathy. In the first nine months of 2026 it was one of the few bright lines in Porsche’s numbers, deliveries up, the old idea still working. People want the shape. They want the noise. Some of them, astonishingly, still want to change gear themselves, like it is a hobby and not a job the car should do while you panic.

I will not be buying a manual 911. I will be aiming, stubbornly, at a Taycan, which has no gears to miss and no clutch to ride. If E-Shift wants to pretend, it can pretend. My left foot is already employed. It rocks a crib.

Not a road test. I have not driven these cars. This is a diary from a man raising money, one euro at a time, for a Taycan by 2 August 2027. Not affiliated with Porsche AG.
