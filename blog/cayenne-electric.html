They made the Cayenne electric and my driveway got nervous

24 April 2026 · by the Portuguese dad who still wants a Taycan

The Cayenne was already the Porsche for people who have things to carry and no interest in explaining themselves. Then Porsche built an electric one, and later a coupe version that sits next to the petrol and the plug-in hybrid, like a family that refuses to pick a favourite child.

By the autumn it had a few thousand owners. Not a rumour. A car you can order, configure, and disappoint your accountant with. Porsche even came back in September to talk about more colours and more comfort, which is how you know a model has left the press-launch dream and entered the “what stitch do you want on the seat” years.

I do not have a driveway long enough for the drama, but I have the drama. A Cayenne Electric is a school-run weapon disguised as a family apology. You arrive at the gate silent, which in Portugal still counts as suspicious, and then you leave silent, which counts as rich.

It is not my car. Mine has to be the Taycan, because a childhood promise does not get renegotiated just because a taller car makes more sense. Sense is how you end up with a sensible life. I already have one of those. It is called Tuesday.

Not a road test. I have not driven these cars. This is a diary from a man raising money, one euro at a time, for a Taycan by 2 August 2027. Not affiliated with Porsche AG.
