The Macan Electric is winning and I am trying to be mature about it

9 October 2026 · by the Portuguese dad who still wants a Taycan

Here is the humiliating truth. The Porsche that normal families are actually buying is not the one on my mood board.

Through September 2026, Porsche had handed over about 23,000 Macan Electrics. The Taycan, my chosen personality, managed under 9,000 in the same nine months, and that number was down hard on the year before. More than one in five Porsches delivered worldwide in that stretch was fully electric. The Macan did most of the work.

Of course it did. It is the car you can explain to a spouse. School run. Boot. A plug. A badge that still makes the neighbour look up from his hedge. I have a three-year-old and an eight-month-old. I should want the Macan. I want the low one that looks like it disapproves of speed bumps.

The petrol Macan is on the way out. The electric one is the front door of the brand now. Good for them. Bad for my ability to pretend I am a rational economic actor.

If you came here to compare SUVs and stayed to send a euro to a man having a public crisis, the donation buttons are on the homepage. The Macan can wait. My birthday cannot. That is a lie. Birthdays always arrive. The Taycan is the part that needs help.

Not a road test. I have not driven these cars. This is a diary from a man raising money, one euro at a time, for a Taycan by 2 August 2027. Not affiliated with Porsche AG.
