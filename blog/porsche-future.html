Porsche is planning a car above the 911 and I am still planning Tuesday

7 October 2026 · by the Portuguese dad who still wants a Taycan

In October the boss, Michael Leiters, stood in Weissach and described a five-year plan that includes a super-sports car clearly above the 911. Mid-engined. A descendant, in spirit, of the Carrera GT and the 918. The sort of sentence that makes a forum refresh itself.

There is more. A flagship SUV with seven seats, for people whose family outgrew a Cayenne and whose pride did not. A petrol SUV to replace the Macan that is leaving, shared in its bones with Audi, aimed at 2028. And the electric 718, which he has apparently driven hard enough to stop pretending electric sports cars are a theory.

I read all of this from a flat where the most powerful object is the kettle. Porsche is designing a ladder I am not on. That is fine. Ladders are for people with a free hand. I am carrying a child and a dream and, on a good month, the electricity bill.

The miracle, if it comes, is not a one-off hypercar. It is a lot of strangers deciding that one euro is a funny thing to spend. The buttons are on the homepage. Quantity on PayPal’s own link only goes as high as PayPal allows, which is less dramatic than a hypercar and more dramatic than my savings account.

If nobody comes, the charity ending still stands. If somebody comes with a ridiculous amount, I will assume the universe misclicked and I will not talk them out of it.

Not a road test. I have not driven these cars. This is a diary from a man raising money, one euro at a time, for a Taycan by 2 August 2027. Not affiliated with Porsche AG.
