The Taycan learned to pretend it has gears

18 June 2026 · by the Portuguese dad who still wants a Taycan

Porsche looked at the Taycan, the car I have been emotionally financing since before my daughter could sit up, and decided it was too quiet about its feelings.

So the 2027 cars get something called E-Shift. Virtual gears. A kick when it “changes”. A noise, on purpose. An electric car clearing its throat so you remember this is still a Porsche and not a very fast appliance.

I respect it. I also do not have the car. I have a progress bar and two children who believe the vacuum is a dragon. If a stranger sends one euro, the Taycan does not feel it. I do. That is the whole religion of this page.

People who already own one will argue about whether fake gears are honest. I am not in that argument. I am in the argument where the school shoes cost more than I budgeted and the dream is still electric, still silver in my head, still scheduled for my birthday on 2 August 2027.

If they add theatre, fine. I will take the theatre. I would also take a used one with a scratch on the bumper and no theatre at all. Dignity is a luxury trim. I did not tick it.

Not a road test. I have not driven these cars. This is a diary from a man raising money, one euro at a time, for a Taycan by 2 August 2027. Not affiliated with Porsche AG.
