The (Not So) Epic Story

Hi. I’m a completely ordinary Portuguese guy. 41 years old. House, wife, a 3-year-old boy and an 8-month-old girl. My business is the kind that barely covers the bills on a good month. Nothing historically relevant has ever happened in my life. No viral moments, no lottery wins, no sudden fame.

What I do have is a childhood dream that never left: owning a Porsche. Not a museum piece. Not a race car. Just a proper Porsche that I can drive with my kids in the back and smile like an idiot every time I get behind the wheel.

Specifically, I want a Porsche Taycan — the electric one. Quiet, fast, and somehow still unmistakably Porsche. My birthday is August 2, 2027. That’s the deadline. If enough people buy me an espresso, I might actually make it.

The idea is simple: one espresso per person. €1, $1 or £1. If 100,000 kind (or slightly chaotic) humans each buy me one, we get there. On paper, that makes me the most caffeinated man in Europe. In reality I won’t drink a single one. Every espresso goes straight into the Taycan jar. No complicated tiers. No stretch goals that involve buying a yacht. Just the car.

Buy Me an Espresso

In Portugal a proper espresso costs about a euro and is drunk standing up in under a minute. So pretend you’re at the counter with me. You say “uma bica, por favor”, you pay, I say thank you, and the cup quietly turns into a tiny piece of a Taycan.

PayPal only, so the payment goes through a company you already know. If you want, write your name or a joke in the PayPal note, or tell me how you take it: short, long, with a drop of milk. I’ll read every one.

What Happens If We Don’t Make It?

If by August 2, 2027 the total isn’t enough to buy a Taycan (even a used one), the money will go to the fight against cancer in Portugal, through the Liga Portuguesa Contra o Cancro (the Portuguese Cancer League). And if we do make it, every euro above €100,000 goes to the League too. The car has a ceiling; the kindness doesn’t. Either way, it ends well for somebody. No disappearing acts. No “the money went to my mortgage.” Just a clear fallback.

This page is not run by or affiliated with the Liga Portuguesa Contra o Cancro.

This is not a scam. I’m not promising anyone a ride, a share of the car, or eternal glory. Nobody gets an actual espresso either, including me. I’m just a regular guy asking strangers to help turn a silly lifelong dream into something real. If it works, it’s a great story. If it doesn’t, at least a good cause benefits.
