The (Not So) Epic Story

Hi. I’m a completely ordinary Portuguese guy. 41 years old. House, wife, a 3-year-old boy and an 8-month-old girl. My business is the kind that barely covers the bills on a good month. Nothing historically relevant has ever happened in my life. No viral moments, no lottery wins, no sudden fame.

What I do have is a childhood dream that never left: owning a Porsche. Not a museum piece. Not a race car. Just a proper Porsche that I can drive with my kids in the back and smile like an idiot every time I get behind the wheel.

Specifically, I want a Porsche Taycan — the electric one. Quiet, fast, and somehow still unmistakably Porsche. My birthday is August 2, 2027. That’s the deadline. If enough people throw in the price of a coffee, I might actually make it.

What Happens If We Don’t Make It?

If by August 2, 2027 the total isn’t enough to buy a Taycan (even a used one), the money will go to a charity of my choice. I’ll announce which one well before the deadline so it’s transparent. No disappearing acts. No “the money went to my mortgage.” Just a clear fallback.

This is not a scam. I’m not promising anyone a ride, a share of the car, or eternal glory. I’m just a regular guy asking strangers to help turn a silly lifelong dream into something real. If it works, it’s a great story. If it doesn’t, at least a good cause benefits.
